## Persona 4 — the trainer who has to deliver it cold

This skill's own, because this skill ships a deck and the assessment skill does not.

**The brief.** You are a qualified trainer rostered to deliver this unit tomorrow. You did not write these materials and you have never seen them. You have the deck open on the projector and the speaker notes on your laptop. **The learner guide is not open, and you will not have time to open it.**

Work through one Topic slide by slide and answer:

- **Does each slide's note say what the point is?** A note that restates the slide's bullets tells you nothing you cannot already see. It must say what you are meant to explain, and what the learner is meant to leave with.
- **Does the note say which question this prepares?** A trainer who cannot see the assessment link cannot tell the room why the next fifteen minutes matter, and the room can tell.
- **Can you answer the obvious question from the note alone?** The one a learner asks every time — *"do we have to remember the number?"*, *"what happens if it fails?"*, *"is that the law or is that just here?"* If the note leaves you guessing, so are you in the room.
- **Does the sequence hold?** Can you get from this slide to the next without a fact the deck never gave you?
- **Where a figure appears on a slide, does the note say where it comes from and what weight it carries** — the Code, the unit, the venue's own procedure? A trainer who presents a venue standard as the law teaches the room something false, and the deck is where that happens, because a slide has no room for the qualification the guide has.
- **Is the timing honest?** A Topic that claims a session and needs two is a delivery defect the trainer discovers in front of the class.

**A slide whose note does not say what the point is and which question it prepares fails.** That is the persona's floor, and it is not negotiable against slide count.

**Report per slide, by number.** A finding without a slide number cannot be remediated.

## Reading the results

Same as upstream. A finding that two personas raise independently is not two findings, it is one with corroboration, and it goes to the top of Stage 7's list. A finding only the student raises is still a finding — the student owns the form.

**Persona 4's findings bind the deck's speaker notes specifically.** They are not satisfied by fixing the guide, because the trainer never opens it.
