# The resource production loop (P-01)

Mini-cycle of P-00, the product cycle, in *Operating Cycles and Registers*. Turns once per unit and again for every version. Live page: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/5c37e875-ee9a-4304-9be8-510f91fbca38. Registers: Google Drive > Registers.

## Eleven steps

| Step | Stage | What happens | Who | Writes | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | — | Five AI uses registered with review dates: curriculum registry, assessment drafting, learner-resource drafting, artwork generation, clean-room verifier | ICT & AI, once | AI system register (kept elsewhere) | Five rows live, none past review |
| R1 | 01 | Work order: the tool rows at 0.1 Draft — unit and release read that day, builder, skills, the three names | The command writes; the head of training enters | Tool register | Rows exist before anything is staged |
| R2 | 01 | Build: registry brief → assessment pack (+ workbook for food) → learner guide + deck, from that pack | The command, for the builder | nothing in a register | Brief with no blocker; every skill gate green |
| R3 | 01 | Clean-room verification: a separate agent with the documents and the unit only checks every E, PC, PE, KE and AC on training.gov.au, and the guide and deck against the pack | The command | — (report attached at R5) | A dated discrepancy list per document, naming the release |
| R4 | 01 | Rebuild until clean, max three rounds; each item fixed through the skill or answered with a written reason | The command | — | Nothing unresolved |
| R5 | 02 | Staging package: deliverables + work order, run log, reports, verification → Drive > Staging; rows to Staged with links | The command packages; the head of training moves it | Tool register | Rows at Staged with attachments |
| R6 | 03 | Trainer review, one sitting, pack + guide + deck: current practice, deliverability | The trainer who will deliver, with currency on file, not the builder | Industry consultation register | Change column filled, currency linked |
| R7 | 04 · gate | Pre-use review, pack only: four principles, four rules, practical components; validity is the human check against the unit using the AI report as the worklist | Same trainer, same sitting; holds the unit; not the builder | Pre-use tool review record | Row dated before approval and first use |
| R8 | 05 | Correct after review: new 0.x through the command, R3–R5 again, then the sitting again | The builder re-runs | Tool register (version) | Every finding closed on its row |
| R9 | 05 · gate | Approve and version: pack first, then guide and deck with the pack version; previous version superseded, kept | The approver — never the builder, never the trainer | Tool register | Approver and date on every row |
| R10 | 06 | Publish to eSkilled under the version; TAS tools-in-use updated; AI-use statement per task | Head of training | Tool register; the TAS | eSkilled version equals the row's |

## Three registers

- **Assessment tool register** — Standards 1.3(a), 1.4 and document control. One row per document per version. Written R1, R5, R8, R9, R10; read at every gate.
- **Pre-use tool review record** — Standard 1.3(b). One row per assessment tool per version, before first use, by someone other than the author. Written R7; read R9.
- **Industry consultation register** — Standard 1.2(b),(c): advice and the change it produced. Written R6; read R9.

Attachments to a tool row, not registers: work order and run log, compliance report, resource report, AI verification report. Read here, kept elsewhere: the TAS, the credential matrix, the PD register (industry currency), the curriculum registry, the AI system register, the improvement register.

## Three people per unit, never fewer

| Builder | Delivering trainer | Reviews (R6, R7) | Approves (R9) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daman | a trainer other than the head of training | that trainer | Head of training |
| Daman | the head of training | the head of training | nobody is left — a staff member or ICT & AI builds instead, and Daman approves |
| a staff member or ICT & AI | any trainer, HoT included | that trainer | Daman |

Two people cannot do it: the approver would be the author or the teacher.

## Versions

0.x draft (the command produces nothing else) · 1.0 first approval · 1.x rework on the same release, the whole gate again · 2.0 new release or structural change, new rows linked supersedes / superseded by · guide and deck carry their own version and the pack version they derive from.

## What reopens a version

Review finding before approval (→ R8); validation finding, marking-time defect, industry advice (→ improvement register → R1/R2 as a new version); training.gov.au changes the unit (→ new release, new rows); a registry ruling moves (→ re-read the brief); templates or brand change (→ at each unit's next version).
