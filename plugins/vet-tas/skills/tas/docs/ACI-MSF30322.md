### The rulings in full

#### MSF30322-T01 &mdash; Reporting hazards, incidents and injuries

*progressive-depth* &middot; taught in full by **CPCWHS1001**

**Why here.** Both construction WHS units sit in Cluster 1. CPCWHS1001 is the White Card statutory induction and is the first thing a learner does; CPCCWHS2001 follows it in the same cluster with the organisational procedure.

**What the other units do.** CPCCWHS2001 teaches only the delta - the employer's own procedure, the register, and the notifiable-incident duty owed to the regulator.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCWHS1001 KE: identifying and reporting hazards, incidents and injuries in the workplace

#### MSF30322-T02 &mdash; Workplace health and safety procedures in a furniture workshop

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM2013**

**Why here.** Six units name workplace WHS procedures. The construction WHS units in Cluster 1 own the LEGAL frame; MSFFM2013 Use furniture making hand and power tools, first in Cluster 3, owns the workshop-floor procedure - machine guarding, isolation, housekeeping, dust - because that is the first unit where a learner picks up a tool.

**What the other units do.** Later units name the procedure that governs their own operation and the specific risk it controls. None of them restate the general duty, which belongs to Cluster 1.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM2013 KE: workplace health and safety procedures

#### MSF30322-T03 &mdash; Personal protective equipment for furniture and cabinetry work

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM2013**

**Why here.** Seven units name PPE. Every one of them names the SAME equipment for a different operation - eye, hearing, respiratory and hand protection against the same timber dust, noise and blade hazards. Teaching it seven times teaches nothing new six of those times.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM2013 teaches selection, fit, inspection and care once. Every later unit states which items its operation requires and why, in a line, and is assessed on WEARING them rather than on knowing what they are.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM2013 KE: personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements for hand and power tools used in furniture production.

#### MSF30322-T04 &mdash; Reporting machinery faults and material defects

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM2016**

**Why here.** A fault report is one procedure - tag out, report, do not use. MSFFM2016 Join solid timber is the earliest of the three units in Cluster 3 to require it.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM2018 and MSFFM3042 identify the faults and defects specific to their own machines and materials. The reporting procedure itself is cited.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM2016 KE: reporting of machinery faults and material defects

#### MSF30322-T05 &mdash; Workplace records, reporting and the legislation behind them

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSMSUP106**

**Why here.** Two families - record keeping requirements, and the legislative requirements attaching to work activities - are one topic in Cluster 1: what has to be written down, and which instrument requires it. MSMSUP106 Work in a team is delivered in Cluster 1 and carries organisational procedure as its own subject matter.

**What the other units do.** MSFFF2012 records its own surface-preparation results to that format. MSFFDM4002 identifies the specific legislation bearing on production processes, and does not restate what legislation is.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSMSUP106 KE: organisation procedures, including:record keeping requirements
- MSMSUP106 EL: 1. Identify work activities

#### MSF30322-T06 &mdash; Characteristics, features and components of cabinetry products

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM3041**

**Why here.** MSFFM3041 Determine and document requirements for cabinetry installation is delivered first in Cluster 6 and must name the components before it can specify them. MSFFM3042 Fabricate cabinetry then builds them.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM3042 teaches how each component is MADE - the machining, the edge treatment, the assembly - not what it is called or what it is for.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM3041 KE: characteristics and features of cabinetry products and components

#### MSF30322-T07 &mdash; Door and drawer systems, and their alignment

*progressive-depth* &middot; taught in full by **MSFKB2003**

**Why here.** MSFKB2003 Prepare for cabinetry installation, in Cluster 6, owns the hardware - hinge types, runner types, how each is specified and set out. MSFFM3043 Install cabinetry, in Cluster 7, owns adjusting them true on site, which is a different skill and is genuinely new.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM3043 does not re-teach hardware selection. It teaches the three-way adjustment and the sequence for bringing a run of doors into line.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFKB2003 KE: door and drawer systems

#### MSF30322-T08 &mdash; Reading the building: wall and floor construction and out-of-square conditions

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM3041**

**Why here.** A cabinetmaker measures the room before making anything, and again before fixing anything. MSFFM3041 in Cluster 6 owns it because the survey is what its documentation is FOR.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM3043 applies the scribing and packing techniques that an out-of-square condition demands, and cites MSFFM3041 for how the condition was identified.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM3041 KE: angles of floor to walls

#### MSF30322-T09 &mdash; Statutory planning regulations affecting cabinetry installation

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **MSFFM3041**

**Why here.** Local and state planning requirements are a single body of regulation. It is explained where a learner first has to check it - at the documentation stage, in MSFFM3041.

**What the other units do.** MSFFM3043 confirms the approval is in place before installing and names the consequence of installing without it. It does not re-explain the planning system.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- MSFFM3041 KE: statutory local and state or territory planning regulations affecting cabinetry installations
