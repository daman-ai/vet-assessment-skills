### The rulings in full

#### SIT50422-T01 &mdash; Professional service standards and the attributes expected of service personnel

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXCCS016**

**Why here.** Two families - professional service standards, and the attitudes and attributes the industry expects - are one topic: what good service looks like and who is expected to deliver it. SITXCCS016 Develop and manage quality customer service practices is the unit that WRITES the standard and is delivered third; SITXCCS015 works to it, delivered fifth.

**What the other units do.** SITXCCS015 opens by naming the standard set in SITXCCS016 and moves straight to its own ground: reading an individual customer, anticipating a need, and recovering a service failure in the moment.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXCCS016 KE: attitudes and attributes expected by the service industries to work with customers
- SITXCCS016 KE: professional service standards for service industry personnel

#### SIT50422-T02 &mdash; Organisational and management restructures as a planning input

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFIN010**

**Why here.** SITXFIN010 Prepare and monitor budgets is delivered second and must treat a restructure as a factor that moves a budget. SITXCCS016, delivered third, treats the same restructure as a factor that dates a service policy. One event, explained once, in the unit that meets it first.

**What the other units do.** SITXCCS016 refers back in a line and teaches only the consequence peculiar to it - that a restructure invalidates the service standards written for the old structure.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFIN010 KE: organisational and management restructures

#### SIT50422-T03 &mdash; Complaint and dispute management

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXCCS016**

**Why here.** The complaint system - how complaints are captured, escalated, resolved and fed back into policy - is designed in SITXCCS016. SITXCCS015 handles the complaint in front of the learner.

**What the other units do.** SITXCCS015 teaches the interaction, not the system: acknowledging, taking ownership, offering a remedy inside delegation. It cites the SITXCCS016 escalation path rather than restating it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXCCS016 KE: complaint and dispute management

#### SIT50422-T04 &mdash; Consulting staff when developing customer service policy

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXCCS016**

**Why here.** Consultation is part of writing the policy, which is SITXCCS016's own work. SITXCCS015 contributes suggestions rather than running the consultation.

**What the other units do.** In SITXCCS015 this is a single line about where a frontline observation goes. The consultation method itself is not repeated.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXCCS016 KE: seeking staff suggestions for content of customer service policies and procedures
