## Themes

**1. Safe work and food hygiene** (weeks 1-8)

Work safely on the bench, control hazards and handle food to food-safety standard.

`SITXWHS005`, `SITXFSA005`, `SITXFSA006`, `SITXWHS006`

**2. Section set-up, stock and basic cookery** (weeks 9-22)

Cost a recipe, order and store goods, run the equipment and clean down, and cook by basic methods.

`SITHKOP010`, `SITXINV007`, `SITHCCC023`, `SITHKOP009`, `SITXINV006`, `SITHCCC027`, `SITXHRM007`

**3. Plated desserts, buffets and dietary work** (weeks 23-31)

Produce and plate hot and cold desserts, set a sweet buffet display and adapt items for dietary needs.

`SITHCCC042`, `SITHCCC038`, `SITHPAT016`

**4. Cakes, pastries and yeast goods** (weeks 32-47)

Bake, fill and finish cakes and gateaux, and produce pastry and yeast-raised bakery lines.

`SITHPAT011`, `SITHPAT012`, `SITHPAT013`, `SITHPAT014`

**5. Petits fours, marzipan and kitchen placement** (weeks 48-58)

Produce petits fours and modelled marzipan work, then hold a section through a full service in a commercial kitchen.

`SITHPAT015`, `SITHPAT017`, `SITHCCC034`

### The rulings in full

#### SIT31021-T01 &mdash; Selecting, assembling and safely operating commercial kitchen and patisserie equipment

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** Eleven units restate safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment, and ten carry the element about preparing food safely with selected equipment. SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment in Theme 2 is the unit whose subject it is, and it is delivered before the whole patisserie stream.

**What the other units do.** Each Theme 4 unit names the equipment its own products need and the settings peculiar to them - a dough sheeter gauge, a deck oven with steam injection, a tempering machine curve - and does not re-teach assembly or safe operation. ONE EXCEPTION, AND IT IS AN ARTEFACT: SITXFSA006 sits in Theme 1, ahead of the owner, and cannot recall what it has not met. The detector chained it into this family on its element Prepare food safely, which is about safe HANDLING rather than equipment operation. SITXFSA006 teaches its own food-safety content in full, including the thermometers and sanitising equipment food safety needs, and nothing here is withheld from it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment used in the performance evidence.
- SITHCCC023 EL: 2. Use equipment to prepare food.

#### SIT31021-T02 &mdash; Mise en place: what it means and why it governs the kitchen

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** The meaning and role of mise en place is stated in SITHCCC023 and again in SITHCCC027. It is one idea and belongs where a learner first organises a workstation.

**What the other units do.** Later units plan their own mise en place and are assessed on the plan working, not on defining the term.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: meaning and role of mise en place in the process of preparing, cooking and presenting food

#### SIT31021-T03 &mdash; Portioning and preparing ingredients to a standard recipe

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC027**

**Why here.** Eight units carry the element Portion and prepare ingredients. SITHCCC027 Prepare dishes using basic methods of cookery opens Theme 3 and is the first cookery unit. Accurate scaling matters more in patisserie than anywhere else, which is why it is taught before Theme 4 rather than inside it.

**What the other units do.** Theme 4 units scale their own formulas and are assessed on accuracy, not on how to weigh.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC027 EL: 3. Portion and prepare ingredients.

#### SIT31021-T04 &mdash; Keeping food safe and saleable in storage: date codes, rotation, freshness, storage conditions and shelf life

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXINV006**

**Why here.** Four families - date codes and rotation labels in nine units, storage conditions in eight, shelf life in eight and freshness indicators in seven - are one topic and the subject of SITXINV006 Receive, store and maintain stock in Theme 2. Thirty-two restatements of how to store food, almost all of them in the seven Theme 4 units saying it about their own product.

**What the other units do.** A patisserie unit states only what is SPECIFIC to its product - that a cream-filled item is a potentially hazardous food, that a baked meringue is hygroscopic, that marzipan is wrapped against drying - and cites SITXINV006 for the storage system and the labelling.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXINV006 KE: contents of date codes and rotation labels for stock

#### SIT31021-T05 &mdash; Organisational hygiene and food safety procedures

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** SITXFSA005 Use hygienic practices for food safety is delivered in Theme 1 precisely because most later units name it as a prerequisite.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 follows the procedure and is assessed on following it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: contents of organisational hygiene and food safety procedures

#### SIT31021-T06 &mdash; Food safety law, the Food Standards Code and the meaning of contamination

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** One account of the Code, of contaminants and potentially hazardous foods, and of the regulator.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 and SITXINV006 name the specific requirement governing their activity and cite the clause rather than re-summarising the Code.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: basic aspects of commonwealth, state or territory food safety laws, standards and codes as follows:meaning of contaminant, contamination and potentially hazardous foods as defined by the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code

#### SIT31021-T07 &mdash; Safe handling of high-risk food types

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA006**

**Why here.** Dairy, eggs and other high-risk types are named in SITXFSA006 Participate in safe food handling practices and again in SITHCCC023. Patisserie runs on cream, custard and egg, so this is load-bearing for the whole qualification.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC023 handles those foods with the equipment it teaches and cites the handling rules.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA006 KE: safe food handling practices for the following different food types:dairy
- SITXFSA006 KE: food safety monitoring techniques:bacterial swabs and counts

#### SIT31021-T08 &mdash; Work health and safety: legislation, duties, hazards and the consequences of failure

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXWHS005**

**Why here.** Five families covering the Act, the duties, the hazards and the penalty are one body of law. SITXWHS005 Participate in safe work practices opens Theme 1.

**What the other units do.** SITXWHS006 teaches the DELTA - risk assessment, hierarchy of control, monitoring - which is new work. The food safety units cite SITXWHS005 for duty, breach and penalty and name the Food Act as their own instrument.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXWHS005 KE: ramifications of failure to observe WHS legislation and organisational policies and procedures
- SITXWHS005 KE: employee responsibilities to participate in WHS practices
- SITXWHS005 KE: workplace hazards and associated health, safety and security risks

#### SIT31021-T09 &mdash; Cleaning products, chemicals and their safe use

*progressive-depth* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** The original ruling gave this to SITHKOP009 Clean kitchen premises and equipment, which carries the deeper content. But SITXFSA005 is delivered in Theme 1 and SITHKOP009 in Theme 2, and SITXFSA005 cannot explain why a surface must be sanitised without naming what sanitises it. A learner cannot recall a chemical they have not met, so the introduction belongs to the earlier unit.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA005 introduces cleaning and sanitising at the level food safety requires - what a sanitiser is, why contact time matters, and that a chemical carries a safety data sheet. SITHKOP009 in Theme 2 then teaches the DELTA, which is substantial and genuinely new: the full product range, dilution ratios, chemical storage and segregation, the PPE each product demands, and reading an SDS end to end.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: using cleaning products or chemicals

#### SIT31021-T10 &mdash; Environmentally sound disposal of kitchen waste

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHKOP009**

**Why here.** Separation, recycling and disposal of broken service-ware are the cleaning unit's own ground in Theme 2.

**What the other units do.** SITXINV006 disposes of out-of-date stock to that procedure and cites it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHKOP009 KE: correct and environmentally sound disposal methods for kitchen waste:broken service-ware

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC042 reads a preference or restriction and acts on it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHKOP010 EL: 1. Identify customer preferences.

#### SIT31021-T12 &mdash; Special dietary requirements: allergies, consequences and substitute ingredients

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC042**

**Why here.** Three families - food allergy types, the health and legal consequences of failing to address a requirement, and the culinary terms for substitute ingredients - are one topic. SITHCCC042 in Theme 3 is delivered before the whole patisserie stream, which matters because gluten, dairy, egg and nut are the four allergens patisserie is built on.

**What the other units do.** SITHPAT016 teaches the dessert-specific substitutions and cites SITHCCC042 for the allergy knowledge and the legal consequence. SITHCCC034 applies it during service.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC042 KE: main types and characteristics of:food allergy
- SITHCCC042 KE: culinary terms and trade names for:substitute ingredients used to produce dishes with special dietary recipes
- SITHCCC042 KE: key health, legal and reputational consequences of failing to address special requirements, including:allergic reactions

#### SIT31021-T13 &mdash; Culinary terms and trade names for ingredients

*commodity-parallel* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC038**

**Why here.** Seven units require the terms for THEIR OWN ingredients. The vocabulary of yeast doughs is not the vocabulary of petits fours. Only the idea that a trade name, a convenience product and a classical term can name one thing is set once, in the earliest unit to require it.

**What the other units do.** Every unit teaches its own product vocabulary in full and is assessed on it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC038 KE: culinary terms and trade names for:ingredients commonly used to produce buffet items

#### SIT31021-T14 &mdash; Fundamental patisserie techniques: sifting, stirring, aerating, whisking, folding, piping, spreading and mould preparation

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHPAT016**

**Why here.** Five families covering the same hand skills appear in six units in identical words. SITHPAT016 Produce desserts opens Theme 4 and is where a learner first performs them. This is the largest single duplication in the patisserie stream: five techniques restated up to six times each.

**What the other units do.** Each later Theme 4 unit applies the techniques and teaches only what its own product adds - gluten development in SITHPAT014, sugar work in SITHPAT015, tempering and modelling in SITHPAT017.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHPAT016 KE: cookery methods and processes used when preparing desserts:adding fats and liquids to dry ingredients
- SITHPAT016 KE: sifting dry ingredients
- SITHPAT016 KE: stirring and aerating to achieve required consistency and texture

**What the other units do.** Later Theme 4 units set up for their own product and are assessed on the setup being right, not on the routine.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHPAT011 KE: mise en place requirements for producing cakes, sponges and fillings

- SITHPAT011 KE: preparing and using pre-bake finishes and decorations

#### SIT31021-T24 &mdash; Chilling ingredients and work surfaces for pastry work

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHPAT013**

- SITHPAT013 KE: chilling ingredients and work surfaces

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHPAT012 KE: cigarette paste decoration techniques
