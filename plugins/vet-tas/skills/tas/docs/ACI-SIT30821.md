**Adelaide Culinary Institute** &middot; RTO 45797 &middot; CRICOS 03978F &middot; Certificate III &middot; 52 weeks

> **The other institute delivers this qualification too, from its own strategy.** See `MVC-SIT30821`. The two hold SEPARATE registers: their elective selections and their delivery orders differ, and a different elective set changes which unit owns a shared topic. Never read one register for the other.


25 units - 20 core plus 5 electives; 3 from Group A or B and 2 from Group A, B or C. 25 units are recorded; **25 are delivered**.

> 52 weeks: 48 weeks face to face, 4 weeks term break, and 48 service periods of work placement.

> ACI AND MERIDIAN SELECT THE IDENTICAL 25 UNITS for this qualification - confirmed by comparing the two strategies, not assumed. The two records still differ because the DELIVERY ORDER differs: Meridian groups its units into four themes, ACI lists a straight sequence.

## Open items

- CLOSED 8 SEPTEMBER 2026 - this register previously asked whether the ACI strategy's 1-to-25 numbering was a teaching order or a listing order, because three rulings turned on the answer. The question is now moot. The course is sequenced from its constraints rather than from the numbering: every prerequisite precedes the unit that needs it and every topic owner precedes the units that recall it, and ACI now carries the identical six themes as Meridian in the identical order. The three rulings were resolved on the units' knowledge evidence, not on the numbering.

## Themes

**1. Safe kitchens, food safety and recipe costing** (weeks 1-8)

Work safely, control food safety hazards, cost recipes and order goods for the kitchen.

`SITXWHS005`, `SITXWHS006`, `SITXHRM007`, `SITHKOP010`, `SITXINV007`, `SITXFSA005`, `SITXFSA006`

**2. Kitchen set-up, stock and basic cookery** (weeks 9-15)

Set up, use and clean commercial equipment, control stock, and cook dishes using the basic methods of cookery.

`SITHCCC023`, `SITHKOP009`, `SITXINV006`, `SITHCCC027`

**3. Desserts, special diets and buffet work** (weeks 16-24)

Produce desserts, appetisers, salads and buffet dishes, and adjust them for special dietary, vegetarian and vegan requirements.

`SITHPAT016`, `SITHCCC042`, `SITHCCC028`, `SITHCCC038`, `SITHCCC031`

**4. Stocks, sauces and kitchen staples** (weeks 25-32)

Produce stocks, sauces and soups, vegetable, egg and farinaceous dishes, and cook-chill and cook-freeze items safely.

`SITHCCC029`, `SITHCCC030`, `SITHCCC032`

**5. Poultry, meat and seafood** (weeks 33-42)

Select, portion and cook poultry, meat and seafood dishes to order at commercial speed.

`SITHCCC035`, `SITHCCC036`, `SITHCCC037`

**6. Bakery, specialised items and work placement** (weeks 43-52)

Produce cakes, pastries, breads and specialised food items, then work full services as a cook in industry placement.

`SITHCCC041`, `SITHCCC044`, `SITHCCC043`

### The rulings in full

#### SIT30821-T01 &mdash; Selecting, assembling and safely operating commercial kitchen equipment

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** Fifteen of the twenty-five units carry a knowledge-evidence point reading safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment, changing only the dish it names, and fourteen carry the element Select, prepare and use equipment. SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment, in the second block, is the unit whose entire subject this is. Teaching it fifteen times teaches nothing new fourteen of those times and inflates every downstream assessment tool with a question already answered. The detector suggested SITHKOP009 because it appears first in the unit table, but SITHKOP009 Clean kitchen premises and equipment owns CLEANING equipment, which is a different topic.

**What the other units do.** Every later cookery unit restates in a few lines the equipment its own dishes need and any setting peculiar to them - a blast chiller programme, a combi oven mode, a pastry sheeter gauge - prompts the learner to name the checks they would make before use and what they would do if one failed, then teaches that equipment in full. It does not re-explain assembly, safe operation or the manufacturer instruction from scratch. Its tool still evidences the equipment requirement in full - applied, inside a question about its own dishes or carried by an observation item, against SITHCCC023's benchmark. SITXFSA006 is outside this ruling: it teaches the temperature probe and the cleaning and pest-control materials as food-safety instruments in its own right, and owes SITHCCC023 nothing.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment used in the performance evidence.
- SITHCCC023 EL: 2. Use equipment to prepare food.

#### SIT30821-T04 &mdash; Keeping food safe and saleable in storage: date codes, rotation, freshness, storage conditions and shelf life

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXINV006**

**Why here.** Four families - date codes and rotation labels in thirteen units, environmental storage conditions in eleven, optimising shelf life in ten, and freshness and quality indicators in ten - are one topic, and it is the subject of SITXINV006 Receive, store and maintain stock in the second block. This is the single largest source of repetition in the qualification: forty-four restatements of how to store food, spread across twelve cookery units that each say it about their own ingredient.

**What the other units do.** A cookery unit restates the storage rule in a few lines, states what is SPECIFIC to its commodity - that fish is stored on ice and used within a day, that a cooked custard is a potentially hazardous food, that yeast dough is retarded not frozen - prompts the learner to recall how they would date and rotate it, then teaches its own handling in full. It does not re-explain the rotation system or the labelling scheme from scratch; it cites SITXINV006 for the frame. Its tool still evidences the storage requirement in full - applied to its own commodity, against SITXINV006's benchmark so the standard matches.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXINV006 KE: contents of date codes and rotation labels for stock

**What the other units do.** Each unit covers the origins of the dishes in its own performance evidence. None repeats another's.

*commodity-parallel* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC028**
