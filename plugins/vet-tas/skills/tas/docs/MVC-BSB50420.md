### The rulings in full

#### BSB50420-T01 &mdash; Confidentiality of information and the policies that govern it

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **BSBCMM511**

**Why here.** Three units require the learner to handle information in accordance with organisational confidentiality policy - communicating with influence, managing workplace relationships, and collaborating through digital tools. BSBCMM511 Communicate with influence opens the course and is where a learner first has to judge what may be said and to whom.

**What the other units do.** BSBLDR523 applies it to a relationship or a grievance and BSBTEC404 to what may be posted in a shared digital workspace. Neither re-teaches what the policy is or why it binds.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- BSBCMM511 KE: relevant organisational policies and procedures on confidentiality of information.
- BSBCMM511 KE: organisational policies and procedures relevant to:presenting and negotiating

#### BSB50420-T02 &mdash; Managing resource acquisition

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **BSBOPS502**

**Why here.** BSBOPS502 Manage business operational plans is delivered fourth and acquires resources as part of executing a plan. BSBOPS501 Manage business resources, delivered eleventh, carries the same element. The acquisition process - specify, source, approve, receive - is one process taught where it is first needed.

**What the other units do.** BSBOPS501 teaches what is new to it: the resource lifecycle beyond acquisition - allocation, monitoring of use, maintenance and disposal - and cites BSBOPS502 for the acquisition step.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- BSBOPS502 EL: 2. Manage resource acquisition
