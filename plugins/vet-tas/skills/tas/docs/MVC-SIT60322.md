## Themes

**1. Business plans, finance and assets** (weeks 1-12)

Write and implement a business plan, manage organisational finances and plan the venue's physical assets.

`BSBOPS601`, `BSBFIN601`, `SITXFIN011`

**2. Recruitment and staff performance** (weeks 13-21)

Recruit, select and induct staff, then monitor and manage their performance.

`SITXHRM010`, `SITXHRM012`

**3. Marketing and WHS systems** (weeks 22-30)

Develop and run a marketing strategy and establish a venue-wide work health and safety system.

`SITXMPR014`, `SITXWHS008`

### The rulings in full

#### SIT60322-T01 &mdash; Collecting and acting on customer and staff feedback

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFIN011**

**Why here.** SITXFIN011 Manage physical assets is delivered third and SITXWHS008 seventh. Feedback gathering is a single management technique - surveys, consultation, complaint data - and the asset unit is the first to require it. Teaching it again in the WHS system unit would restate the method and change only the subject it is pointed at.

**What the other units do.** SITXWHS008 recalls the method in one sentence and spends its words on what is DIFFERENT: consultative arrangements that the WHS Act requires by name, and the records that prove consultation happened.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFIN011 KE: customer and staff feedback

#### SIT60322-T02 &mdash; Reorganising work practices in response to monitoring

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXHRM012**

**Why here.** SITXHRM012 Monitor staff performance is delivered fifth and owns the loop of observe, evaluate, adjust. SITXWHS008 applies the same loop to safety performance two units later.

**What the other units do.** SITXWHS008 does not re-teach how to reorganise work. It teaches the WHS-specific trigger for doing so - a risk assessment outcome or an incident investigation finding - and cites SITXHRM012 for the method.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXHRM012 KE: reorganising work practices
