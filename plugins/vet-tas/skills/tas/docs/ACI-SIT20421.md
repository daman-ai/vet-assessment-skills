## Themes

**1. Safe work, hygiene and stock** (weeks 1-5)

Work safely in the kitchen, handle food hygienically, and receive, store and rotate stock correctly.

`SITXWHS005`, `SITXFSA005`, `SITXINV006`, `SITXFSA006`

**2. Kitchen equipment and simple dishes** (weeks 6-10)

Select, use and clean commercial equipment and send out simple dishes to order.

`SITHCCC023`, `SITHCCC024`, `SITHKOP009`

**3. Sandwiches, basic cookery and salads** (weeks 11-17)

Apply the basic methods of cookery and produce sandwiches, appetisers and salads to service standard.

`SITHCCC025`, `SITHCCC027`, `SITHCCC028`

**4. Stocks, sauces and kitchen service** (weeks 18-24)

Produce stocks, sauces, soups and vegetable, egg and farinaceous dishes while working as part of a kitchen brigade.

`SITHCCC029`, `SITHCCC030`, `SITHCCC034`

### The rulings in full

#### SIT20421-T01 &mdash; Selecting and safely operating commercial kitchen equipment

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** Nine of the thirteen units carry a knowledge point reading safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment, and six carry an element about using equipment to prepare food. SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment is delivered second, before every cookery unit, and it is the unit whose whole subject this is.

**What the other units do.** Each later cookery unit restates in a line or two the equipment its own dishes need and any setting peculiar to them, prompts the learner to name the checks they would make before use, then teaches that equipment in full. It does not re-explain assembly, safe operation or the manufacturer instruction from scratch. Its tool still evidences the equipment requirement in full - applied, inside a question about its own dishes or carried by an observation item, against the owner's benchmark. SITXFSA006 is not a recaller of this topic: it teaches its own small equipment requirement in full - calibrating, using, cleaning and fault-checking a temperature probe, and choosing cleaning and sanitising equipment and materials to manufacturer advice - and is assessed on those.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment used in the performance evidence.
- SITHCCC023 EL: 2. Use equipment to prepare food.
