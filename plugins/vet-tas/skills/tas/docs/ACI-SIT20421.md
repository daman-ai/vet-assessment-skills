**Adelaide Culinary Institute** &middot; RTO 45797 &middot; CRICOS 03978F &middot; Certificate II &middot; 24 weeks

### The rulings in full

#### SIT20421-T01 &mdash; Selecting and safely operating commercial kitchen equipment

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** Nine of the thirteen units carry a knowledge point reading safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment, and six carry an element about using equipment to prepare food. SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment is delivered second, before every cookery unit, and it is the unit whose whole subject this is.

**What the other units do.** Each later cookery unit names the equipment its own dishes need and any setting peculiar to them, in a line or two. It does not re-teach assembly, safe operation or the manufacturer instruction, and its assessment tool does not re-ask them.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment used in the performance evidence.
- SITHCCC023 EL: 2. Use equipment to prepare food.

#### SIT20421-T02 &mdash; Mise en place: what it means and why it governs the kitchen

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** The meaning and role of mise en place is stated in three units. It belongs where a learner first organises a workstation, which is SITHCCC023 in week two.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC025 and SITHCCC027 plan their own mise en place and are assessed on the plan working, not on defining the term.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: meaning and role of mise en place in the process of preparing, cooking and presenting food

#### SIT20421-T03 &mdash; Keeping food safe and saleable in storage: date codes, rotation, storage conditions, shelf life and freshness

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXINV006**

**Why here.** Four families - date codes and rotation labels in six units, environmental storage conditions in five, optimising shelf life in five, and freshness and quality indicators in four - are one topic, and it is the subject of SITXINV006 Receive, store and maintain stock, delivered fourth. Twenty restatements of how to store food across a thirteen-unit certificate.

**What the other units do.** A cookery unit states only what is SPECIFIC to its own commodity - that a dressed salad holds badly, that a filled sandwich is a potentially hazardous food - and cites SITXINV006 for the storage system, the rotation method and the labelling.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXINV006 KE: contents of date codes and rotation labels for stock

#### SIT20421-T04 &mdash; Portioning and preparing ingredients to a standard recipe

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC027**

**Why here.** Four units carry the element Portion and prepare ingredients. SITHCCC027 Prepare dishes using basic methods of cookery is the first of them and the foundational cookery unit of the qualification.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC028, SITHCCC029 and SITHCCC030 portion their own commodity to their own recipe. The technique is applied, not retaught.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC027 EL: 3. Portion and prepare ingredients.

#### SIT20421-T05 &mdash; Work health and safety: legislation, duties and the consequences of failure

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXWHS005**

**Why here.** Three families - the ramifications of failing to observe legislation, employee responsibilities, and safe work practices for a job role - are one body of law and duty. SITXWHS005 Participate in safe work practices is delivered first, in week one.

**What the other units do.** The food safety units cite SITXWHS005 for the structure of duty, breach and penalty, and name the Food Act as their own instrument.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXWHS005 KE: ramifications of failure to observe WHS legislation and organisational policies and procedures
- SITXWHS005 KE: employee responsibilities to participate in WHS practices
- SITXWHS005 KE: safe work practices for individual job roles

#### SIT20421-T06 &mdash; Food safety law, the Food Standards Code and the meaning of contamination

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** One account of the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, of what a contaminant and a potentially hazardous food are, and of the regulator. Three units carry it; SITXFSA005 is delivered third and is where it belongs.

**What the other units do.** SITXINV006 and SITXFSA006 name the specific requirement governing their activity - a temperature, a record, a disposal rule - and cite the Code clause rather than re-summarising the Code.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: basic aspects of commonwealth, state or territory food safety laws, standards and codes as follows:meaning of contaminant, contamination and potentially hazardous foods as defined by the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code

#### SIT20421-T07 &mdash; Organisational hygiene and food safety procedures

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** What the organisation's own hygiene procedures contain is SITXFSA005's subject matter, and it precedes SITXFSA006 by four units.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 follows the procedure and is assessed on following it, not on reciting what it contains.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: contents of organisational hygiene and food safety procedures

#### SIT20421-T08 &mdash; Safe handling of different food types

*progressive-depth* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** SITHCCC023 is delivered second and SITXFSA006 seventh, so the equipment unit meets food-type handling first - it cannot teach a learner to use a slicer on cooked meat without saying how that meat is handled. SITXFSA006 Participate in safe food handling practices then covers the full matrix.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC023 introduces handling only as far as its own equipment demands. SITXFSA006 teaches the DELTA and it is substantial: every high-risk food type, cross-contamination between them, and the temperature control each requires.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: food safety practices for handling different food types

#### SIT20421-T09 &mdash; Cleaning products, chemicals and their safe use

*progressive-depth* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** SITXFSA005 is delivered third and SITHKOP009 sixth. SITXFSA005 cannot explain why a surface must be sanitised without naming what sanitises it, and a learner cannot recall a chemical they have not met.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA005 introduces sanitising at the level food safety requires. SITHKOP009 Clean kitchen premises and equipment teaches the DELTA - the product range, dilution ratios, chemical storage, the PPE each demands, and reading a safety data sheet.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: using cleaning products or chemicals

#### SIT20421-T10 &mdash; Environmentally sound disposal of spoilt stock and kitchen waste

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXINV006**

**Why here.** Disposal of stock that has failed a quality check follows directly from the stock control that identifies it, and SITXINV006 is delivered before SITHKOP009.

**What the other units do.** SITHKOP009 disposes of waste generated by cleaning to the same procedure and cites it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXINV006 KE: correct and environmentally sound disposal methods for spoilt stock.

#### SIT20421-T11 &mdash; Processes for the basic cookery methods

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC024**

**Why here.** SITHCCC024 Prepare and present simple dishes is delivered fifth and is the first unit to apply a cookery method. SITHCCC027, delivered ninth, carries the same knowledge point about the processes behind those methods.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC027 extends the range to every method in its own performance evidence and teaches what heat does to each food type, which is new. It does not re-describe what boiling or grilling is.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC024 KE: processes for cookery methods described in the Performance Evidence

#### SIT20421-T12 &mdash; Plating for practicality of service and customer consumption

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC025**

**Why here.** Three units carry plating methods as knowledge evidence. SITHCCC025 Prepare and present sandwiches is the earliest of them and the unit where presentation is most of the product.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC028 and SITHCCC030 plate their own dishes and are judged on the result. Balance, colour, portion appearance and service practicality are taught once.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC025 KE: plating methods for practicality of service and customer consumption

#### SIT20421-T13 &mdash; Judging product appearance and presentation against a specification

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC025**

**Why here.** The vocabulary a cook uses to judge whether a finished item looks right - appearance, presentation, consistency - is one vocabulary, and SITHCCC025 is the earlier of the two units carrying it.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC029 states the characteristics expected of its own stocks, sauces and soups against that vocabulary.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC025 KE: characteristics of different sandwiches specified in the performance evidence:appearance and presentation:balance

#### SIT20421-T14 &mdash; Presenting and storing finished items

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC025**

**Why here.** The element covering presentation and storage of the finished product appears in SITHCCC025 and SITHCCC027, and SITHCCC025 is delivered first.

**What the other units do.** SITHCCC027 presents and stores its own dishes to their own standard.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC025 EL: 3. Present and store sandwiches.

#### SIT20421-T15 &mdash; Classical and contemporary variations of a preparation

*commodity-parallel* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC025**

**Why here.** Variations on a sandwich, an appetiser, a sauce and a farinaceous dish are four different bodies of knowledge sharing one phrase. NOT DUPLICATION - each unit teaches its own.

**What the other units do.** Each unit teaches the variations of its own commodity in full and is assessed on them.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC025 KE: classical and contemporary variations

#### SIT20421-T16 &mdash; Culinary terms and trade names for ingredients

*commodity-parallel* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC028**

**What the other units do.** Each unit teaches its own product vocabulary in full and is assessed on it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC028 KE: culinary terms and trade names for ingredients commonly used in the production of different appetisers and salads
