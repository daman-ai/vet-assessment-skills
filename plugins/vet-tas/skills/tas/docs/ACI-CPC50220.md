## Themes

**1. WHS leadership, risk and contractor legal obligations** (weeks 1-18)

Lead WHS risk management, manage business risk, and administer the contracts and legal obligations of a building contractor.

`BSBWHS513`, `BSBOPS504`, `CPCCBC4003`, `CPCCBC4009`, `CPCCBC5007`

**2. Building codes across building classes** (weeks 19-29)

Apply the NCC and referenced standards to Class 1 and 10, Type C and Type B construction.

`CPCCBC4001`, `CPCCBC4053`, `CPCCBC5001`

**3. Estimating, tendering and cost control** (weeks 30-44)

Produce estimates and schedules, prepare and evaluate tender documentation and monitor costing systems on complex projects.

`CPCCBC4004`, `CPCCBC4005`, `CPCCBC4013`, `CPCCBC5002`

**4. Site supervision and construction management** (weeks 45-60)

Supervise site administration and planning, engage and manage subcontractors and run construction work to program.

`CPCCBC4008`, `CPCCBC5003`, `CPCCBC5005`, `CPCCBC5010`

**5. Structural principles, documentation and set-out** (weeks 61-84)

Apply structural principles to buildings up to three storeys, interpret and produce project documentation, prepare technical reports and control site set-out.

`CPCCBC5018`, `CPCCBC4012`, `CPCCBC4010`, `CPCCBC4014`, `CPCCBC5013`, `CPCCBC4018`

**6. Environmental, quality and business management** (weeks 85-104)

Manage environmental and sustainability practices, project quality and stakeholders, and the finances of a building business.

`CPCCBC5011`, `CPCSUS5001`, `BSBPMG532`, `BSBPMG538`, `CPCCBC5019`

### The rulings in full

#### CPC50220-T01 &mdash; The risk management process

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **BSBWHS513**

**Why here.** Cluster 1 is delivered first and holds both risk units. Identify, analyse, evaluate, treat and monitor is one method whether the risk is a fall from height or an insolvent subcontractor. BSBWHS513 is listed first in the cluster.

**What the other units do.** BSBOPS504 Manage business risk applies the same five steps to commercial risk and teaches what is different - risk appetite, insurance, and the financial consequence of a treatment - not the method.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- BSBWHS513 EL: 4. Evaluate effectiveness of WHS risk management process

#### CPC50220-T02 &mdash; Building and construction contracts and the obligations they create

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4009**

**Why here.** Nineteen of the twenty-seven units assume a contract sits behind the work. CPCCBC4009 Apply legal requirements sits in Cluster 2 and explains the contract as a source of obligation. CPCCBC4003 Select, prepare and administer a construction contract owns the mechanics of choosing and administering one; that content overlapped with nothing and is taught there in full.

**What the other units do.** Estimating, site and technical units name the contract term bearing on their own output - a provisional sum, a variation clause, a practical completion date - and cite this topic rather than re-explaining contract law.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4009 KE: building and construction industry contracts

#### CPC50220-T03 &mdash; Work health and safety duties on building and construction projects

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4009**

**Why here.** Seven units carry WHS duties as a legal obligation of the builder. Cluster 1 owns the RISK METHOD; CPCCBC4009 in Cluster 2 owns the DUTY - who holds it, to whom, and what breaching it costs.

**What the other units do.** Site and technical units name the duty their activity triggers and the control that discharges it. Neither the method nor the legislation is restated.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4009 KE: work health and safety (WHS)

#### CPC50220-T04 &mdash; Builder registration and licensing

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4009**

**Why here.** Who may lawfully contract to build, and in what class of work, is stated once in Cluster 2.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5007 cites the licence class and teaches the obligations attaching to holding it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4009 KE: builder registration and licensing

#### CPC50220-T05 &mdash; Organisational policies and procedures governing project work

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4009**

**Why here.** Five units require work done in accordance with internal policy. Cluster 2 is the earliest cluster containing any of them.

**What the other units do.** Later units name the policy governing their own output and do not re-teach what a policy is.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4009 KE: organisational policies and procedures.

#### CPC50220-T06 &mdash; Australian Consumer Law as it applies to building work

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC5007**

**Why here.** Statutory warranties, misleading conduct and consumer guarantees are one body of law, owned in Cluster 2 where the contractor's legal obligations are the subject.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5019 cites the ACL where it bears on deposits, progress claims and refunds.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC5007 KE: Australian Consumer Law (ACL)

#### CPC50220-T07 &mdash; Business licensing and the Australian Business Licence and Information Service

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC5007**

**Why here.** Finding which licences, permits and registrations a job requires is one research task, taught where the contractor's obligations are taught.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5010 confirms approvals are in place before work starts and does not re-teach how to find them.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC5007 KE: Australian Business Licence and Information Service (ABLIS)

#### CPC50220-T08 &mdash; Return to work and rehabilitation obligations

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC5007**

**Why here.** Workers compensation, injury management and the return-to-work duty are a single statutory scheme, owned with the contractor's other legal obligations.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5010 applies it to an injured worker on a live site and cites the scheme.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC5007 KE: return to work and rehabilitation arrangements

#### CPC50220-T09 &mdash; Construction drawings and specifications as the project record

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4003**

**Why here.** Ten units extract information from the same drawing set. CPCCBC4003 in Cluster 2 is the earliest of them, and a contract is administered against the drawings and specification it incorporates.

**What the other units do.** Estimating, codes and technical units take what they need and state which document governs where two disagree. CPCCBC4012 owns the reading skill itself in Cluster 6.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4003 KE: construction drawings and specifications

#### CPC50220-T10 &mdash; The National Construction Code: structure, building classes and types of construction

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Cluster 3 is the codes cluster and is sequenced ahead of everything depending on it. Classification is the Code's first question, and CPCCBC4001 covers Class 1 and 10.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4053 teaches the delta to Class 2 to 9 Type C and CPCCBC5001 the delta to Type B. Neither re-teaches the Code's structure. CPCSUS5001 and CPCCBC5010 cite the Code rather than describing it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: classes of building and types of construction

#### CPC50220-T11 &mdash; Building defects: causes, implications and remedial work

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Defects caused by failure to apply codes, and the remedial work they demand, are one subject and are the reason the codes cluster exists.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4053, CPCCBC5001 and CPCCBC4010 cite defects specific to their own scope and do not restate the taxonomy.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: causes and implications of building defects related to failure of applying building codes and standards to residential and commercial buildings
- CPCCBC4001 KE: extent of remedial work required for various defects cause by inadequate design and application of building codes and practices

#### CPC50220-T12 &mdash; Material properties, limitations and evidence of suitability

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** What a material can do and the certification proving it may lawfully be used are one topic, needed the moment the Code is applied.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5010 and CPCSUS5001 select materials against this knowledge and are assessed on the selection, not on the properties.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: properties, characteristics and limitations of specified building materials and components
- CPCCBC4001 KE: evidence of suitability for building materials and products

#### CPC50220-T13 &mdash; Performance Solutions and Assessment Methods under the NCC

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Deemed-to-Satisfy against Performance Solution is explained once, with the Code.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4053 identifies where a Performance Solution is likely and cites the method.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: Performance Solutions and Assessment Methods

#### CPC50220-T14 &mdash; Building design principles and structural behaviour under load

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Stress, strain, compression and bending appear in the codes units as the reasoning behind a provision, and Cluster 3 precedes Cluster 6.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5018 extends this to multi-storey load paths and member sizing, which is genuinely new.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: basic building design principles and the behaviour of structures under stress, strain, compression, bending or combined actions

#### CPC50220-T15 &mdash; Quality systems and best-practice approaches

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Compliance and quality are checked the same way, so they are taught together in the codes cluster.

**What the other units do.** BSBPMG532 in Cluster 9 builds the quality plan, which is new content, and cites this topic for the underlying idea.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: organisational policies and procedures, quality systems and best practice approaches

#### CPC50220-T16 &mdash; Environmental requirements and sustainability principles

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** The Code's environmental and energy provisions are introduced with the Code in Cluster 3.

**What the other units do.** CPCSUS5001 in Cluster 8 writes the sustainability policy, which is its own work, and cites this topic for the regulatory frame.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: environmental requirements and sustainability principles and concepts

#### CPC50220-T17 &mdash; Below-ground construction methods

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4053**

**Why here.** Footings, basements, retention and waterproofing below ground appear in CPCCBC4053 in Cluster 3, ahead of CPCCBC5018 in Cluster 6.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC5018 applies structural analysis to below-ground elements and cites the construction methods.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4053 KE: below ground construction:methods

**Why here.** Five units close with the same requirement that work follow workplace procedure and meet safety and environmental obligations. Cluster 4 is the earliest cluster holding any of them.

- CPCCBC4004 KE: workplace procedures, workplace safety and environmental requirements.

- CPCCBC4004 KE: Environmental Protection Authority (EPA) regulations
