### The rulings in full

#### SITSS00069-T01 &mdash; Food safety law, the Food Standards Code and the meaning of contamination

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** Both units carry the same account of the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, of what a contaminant and a potentially hazardous food are, and of the role of the local regulator. In a two-unit skill set delivered over four weeks a learner would meet that account twice in a month.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 names the specific requirement governing the handling task in front of the learner - a temperature, a holding time, a record - and cites the Code clause rather than re-summarising the Code.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: basic aspects of commonwealth, state or territory food safety laws, standards and codes as follows:meaning of contaminant, contamination and potentially hazardous foods as defined by the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code

#### SITSS00069-T02 &mdash; Consequences of failing to observe food safety law

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** Prosecution, closure, licence conditions and the personal liability of a food safety supervisor are one account of consequence. It belongs with the law it follows from.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 states the consequence attaching to its own specific breach and cites this topic for the general structure.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: ramifications of failure to observe food safety law and organisational policies and procedures

#### SITSS00069-T03 &mdash; Organisational hygiene and food safety procedures

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA005**

**Why here.** What the organisation's own hygiene and food safety procedures contain is the subject matter of SITXFSA005, and it is delivered first.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA006 follows the procedure and is assessed on following it, not on reciting what it contains.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA005 KE: contents of organisational hygiene and food safety procedures
