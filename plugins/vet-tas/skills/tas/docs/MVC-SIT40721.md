## Themes

**1. Leading a diverse kitchen team** (weeks 1-8)

Lead and develop a mixed brigade, build an inclusive section culture and handle conflict.

`BSBTWK501`, `SITXHRM009`, `SITXCOM010`

**2. Operations, budgets and rostering** (weeks 9-16)

Monitor day-to-day operations, work to a wage and ingredient budget, build rosters and keep WHS practices in place.

`SITXWHS007`, `SITXFIN009`, `SITXMGT004`, `SITXHRM008`

**3. Food safety, planning and special diets** (weeks 17-24)

Write and monitor a food safety program, plan cooking operations and develop recipes for special dietary requirements.

`SITXFSA008`, `SITHKOP012`, `SITHKOP013`

**4. Advanced patisserie and showpieces** (weeks 25-30)

Produce chocolate confectionery, model sugar-based decorations and design and produce sweet showpieces.

`SITHPAT018`, `SITHPAT019`, `SITHPAT020`
