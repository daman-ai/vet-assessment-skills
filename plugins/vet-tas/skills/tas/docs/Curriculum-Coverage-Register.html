What each unit teaches, and what it doesn't

Every course on scope, which of them a student may start at and which follow another, every unit sourced from training.gov.au, and a ruling on who owns each topic that two units in the same course both claim. A unit teaches what it owns and recalls the rest — but assessment is per unit and is never reduced: every unit still evidences all of its own requirements in its own tool. A ruling governs teaching depth and assessment form, not coverage.

A qualification is recorded only where its Training and Assessment Strategy is on file — the unit list and elective selection are the RTO's own decisions and exist nowhere else. Unit titles, prerequisites, elements, knowledge evidence and currency come from training.gov.au. Topic ownership is authored, not inferred; every ruling carries a written reason. A ruling never removes a requirement from a later unit’s assessment tool — each unit is separately certified and evidences its own requirements in full.
