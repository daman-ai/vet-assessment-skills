### The rulings in full

#### CPC40120-T01 &mdash; Work health and safety law, policy and reporting in the building workplace

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4002**

**Why here.** Two families - the organisation's own safety policies and reporting requirements, and the WHS Acts and regulations behind them - are one topic, and CPCCBC4002 Manage work health and safety in the building and construction workplace is the unit whose entire subject it is. Eleven other units restate part of it. The detector did not link CPCCBC4002 into the legislative family because it words the duty differently, which is exactly the failure this register exists to catch: the unit that OWNS a topic can be the one the machine misses.

**What the other units do.** Every other unit names the specific duty its own activity triggers - a safe work method statement before high-risk work, a principal contractor duty on a notifiable incident - and cites CPCCBC4002 for the Act, the regulator and the penalty structure.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4002 KE: organisation’s workplace safety policies and procedures:workplace safety and reporting requirements

#### CPC40120-T02 &mdash; Industry structure and the subcontractor system

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Twelve of the nineteen units assume the learner knows how a building job is resourced. Subject matter points at CPCCBC4007 Plan building and construction work, and that was the original ruling - but the draft timetable delivers CPCCBC4007 in weeks 9-10 and CPCCBC4001 in weeks 3-4, with CPCCBC4053 and CPCCBC4009 also ahead of it. A learner cannot recall a concept they have not met, so it is introduced in the earliest unit that needs it.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4001 introduces the parties and the contractual chain in the terms the Code needs - who certifies, who is responsible for compliance. CPCCBC4007 in weeks 9-10 then teaches resourcing and procurement against that structure, which is genuinely new, and does not re-describe the industry.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: government building legislation, codes and standards:the NCC:Performance Requirements and General Requirements

#### CPC40120-T03 &mdash; Organisational policies and procedures governing project work

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4009**

**Why here.** Six units require work done in accordance with organisational policy. The original ruling used the TAS unit-table order and picked CPCCBC4004; the draft timetable delivers CPCCBC4009 Apply legal requirements in weeks 7-8, twelve weeks earlier, and it is the first unit in the family a learner meets.

**What the other units do.** Estimating, site and quality units name the policy that governs their own output - a tender approval, a variation authority, a dispute escalation - and do not re-teach what a policy is or why it binds.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4009 KE: organisational policies and procedures.

#### CPC40120-T16 &mdash; Site waste and environmental practice as a legal duty

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4002**
