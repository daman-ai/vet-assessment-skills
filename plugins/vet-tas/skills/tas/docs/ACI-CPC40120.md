## Themes

**1. WHS management, building codes and construction law** (weeks 1-10)

Manage site WHS and apply the NCC, standards and legal requirements to Class 1, 10 and Type C work.

`CPCCBC4002`, `CPCCBC4001`, `CPCCBC4053`, `CPCCBC4009`

**2. Work planning, plans and building drawings** (weeks 11-18)

Plan a residential build program and read, interpret and sketch the drawings the job is built from.

`CPCCBC4007`, `CPCCBC4012`, `CPCCBC4014`

**3. Estimating, scheduling and materials procurement** (weeks 19-26)

Produce estimated costs, labour and material schedules, and select, order and store construction materials.

`CPCCBC4004`, `CPCCBC4005`, `CPCCBC4006`

**4. Contract administration and site management** (weeks 27-37)

Select and administer a building contract, run site communication and records, resolve disputes and minimise site waste.

`CPCCBC4003`, `CPCCBC4024`, `CPCCBC4008`, `CPCCBC4021`

**5. Structural principles, set-out and energy efficiency** (weeks 38-46)

Apply structural principles to residential and commercial work, carry out site set-out, and build to energy efficiency requirements.

`CPCCBC4010`, `CPCCBC4018`, `CPCSUS4002`

**6. Business planning and project quality** (weeks 47-52)

Develop a building business plan and apply quality management techniques to a construction project.

`BSBESB407`, `BSBPMG422`

### The rulings in full

#### CPC40120-T01 &mdash; Work health and safety law, policy and reporting in the building workplace

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4002**

**Why here.** Two families - the organisation's own safety policies and reporting requirements, and the WHS Acts and regulations behind them - are one topic, and CPCCBC4002 Manage work health and safety in the building and construction workplace is the unit whose entire subject it is. Eleven other units restate part of it. The detector did not link CPCCBC4002 into the legislative family because it words the duty differently, which is exactly the failure this register exists to catch: the unit that OWNS a topic can be the one the machine misses.

**What the other units do.** Every other unit names the specific duty its own activity triggers - a safe work method statement before high-risk work, a principal contractor duty on a notifiable incident - and cites CPCCBC4002 for the Act, the regulator and the penalty structure.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4002 KE: organisation’s workplace safety policies and procedures:workplace safety and reporting requirements

#### CPC40120-T02 &mdash; Industry structure and the subcontractor system

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Twelve of the nineteen units assume the learner knows how a building job is resourced. Subject matter points at CPCCBC4007 Plan building and construction work, and that was the original ruling - but the draft timetable delivers CPCCBC4007 in weeks 9-10 and CPCCBC4001 in weeks 3-4, with CPCCBC4053 and CPCCBC4009 also ahead of it. A learner cannot recall a concept they have not met, so it is introduced in the earliest unit that needs it.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4001 introduces the parties and the contractual chain in the terms the Code needs - who certifies, who is responsible for compliance. CPCCBC4007 in weeks 9-10 then teaches resourcing and procurement against that structure, which is genuinely new, and does not re-describe the industry.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- CPCCBC4001 KE: government building legislation, codes and standards:the NCC:Performance Requirements and General Requirements

#### CPC40120-T04 &mdash; Using project plans, drawings and specifications as the source of project information

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **CPCCBC4001**

**Why here.** Eight units draw information out of the same drawing set. The original ruling picked CPCCBC4007; the timetable delivers CPCCBC4001 in weeks 3-4 and CPCCBC4053 in weeks 5-6, both ahead of it, and both must read a drawing to apply a Code provision to it. IMPORTANT: CPCCBC4012 Read and interpret plans and specifications, in weeks 15-16, owns the READING SKILL itself - projection, scale, symbols, cross-referencing - and its content overlapped with nothing, so it is taught there in full and is not in this register. **What the other units do.** Codes, estimating and contract units extract what they need and state which document governs where two disagree. Drawing conventions belong to CPCCBC4012 and are not taught earlier.

**What the other units do.** CPCCBC4053 does not re-teach how the Code is structured or what a class is. It teaches the DELTA between Class 1 and 10 and Class 2 to 9 - fire resistance, egress, sound transmission and the provisions that only apply above a certain rise.
