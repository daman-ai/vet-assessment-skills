## Themes

**1. Leading a diverse kitchen team** (weeks 1-8)

Lead and develop a mixed brigade, build an inclusive section culture and handle conflict.

`BSBTWK501`, `SITXHRM009`, `SITXCOM010`

**2. Operations, budgets and rostering** (weeks 9-18)

Monitor day-to-day operations, work to a wage and food budget, build rosters and keep WHS practices in place.

`SITXWHS007`, `SITXFIN009`, `SITXMGT004`, `SITXHRM008`

**3. Food safety, menus and special diets** (weeks 19-30)

Write and monitor a food safety program, plan cooking operations, design and cost a menu, and develop recipes for special dietary requirements.

`SITXFSA008`, `SITHKOP012`, `SITHKOP013`, `SITHKOP015`

### The rulings in full

#### SIT40521-T01 &mdash; Organisational diversity policy

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **BSBTWK501**

**Why here.** BSBTWK501 Lead diversity and inclusion opens the course and is the unit whose entire subject this is. SITXHRM009 Lead and manage people, delivered second, needs the same policy as the frame for recruitment, rostering and performance conversations. THIS TOPIC EXISTS ONLY IN THE MERIDIAN REGISTER - ACI does not carry BSBTWK501 at all, so its kitchen-management learners meet diversity policy nowhere in this qualification.

**What the other units do.** SITXHRM009 applies the policy to a team situation - allocating work fairly, adjusting for a cultural or religious requirement, handling a complaint of exclusion - and cites BSBTWK501 for the policy itself and the legislation behind it.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- BSBTWK501 KE: organisational diversity policy

#### SIT40521-T02 &mdash; Consequences of failing to observe WHS and food safety law

*regulatory-recall* &middot; taught in full by **SITXWHS007**

**Why here.** SITXWHS007 Implement and monitor work health and safety practices is delivered fourth and carries the legislative frame - duty of care, penalties, prosecution, the regulator. SITXFSA008 restates the same consequence structure for food safety law four units later. One account of what happens when a duty holder fails is also one account an auditor has to reconcile.

**What the other units do.** SITXFSA008 names the Food Standards Code and the state food authority as its own instrument and regulator, and cites SITXWHS007 for the general structure of duty, breach and penalty.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXWHS007 KE: ramifications of failure to observe WHS legislation and organisational policies and procedures

#### SIT40521-T03 &mdash; Food production records

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA008**

**Why here.** SITXFSA008 Develop and implement a food safety program is delivered eighth and SITHKOP013 Plan cooking operations ninth. The record is the same document; in the food safety program it is evidence of control, in cooking operations it is a planning input. It is designed once, where it is designed.

**What the other units do.** SITHKOP013 does not re-teach the record's format or its retention period. It teaches reading yesterday's record to plan today's production.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA008 KE: food production records

#### SIT40521-T04 &mdash; Hazard identification and control at critical control points

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITXFSA008**

**Why here.** HACCP is the subject matter of SITXFSA008 and nothing else in this course teaches it. SITHKOP013 schedules production around the control points that unit establishes.

**What the other units do.** SITHKOP013 assumes the critical control points are known and teaches sequencing production so cooling, holding and reheating windows are not breached.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITXFSA008 KE: hazards:control methods for each critical point
