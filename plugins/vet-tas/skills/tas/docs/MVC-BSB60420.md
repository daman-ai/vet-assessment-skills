## Themes

**1. Business planning and leadership practice** (weeks 1-16)

Develop and implement a business plan while leading workplace communication and your own professional development.

`BSBOPS601`, `BSBPEF501`, `BSBXCM501`

**2. Workforce and corporate responsibility strategy** (weeks 17-32)

Contribute to learning and development, workforce planning and corporate social responsibility strategy for the organisation.

`BSBHRM613`, `BSBSUS601`, `BSBHRM614`

**3. Innovation, change and organisational leadership** (weeks 33-52)

Solve complex problems, drive innovation and continuous improvement, and lead organisational change from the top.

`BSBCRT611`, `BSBSTR601`, `BSBLDR601`, `BSBLDR602`
