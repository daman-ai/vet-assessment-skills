### The rulings in full

#### SIT30821-T01 &mdash; Selecting, assembling and safely operating commercial kitchen equipment

*shared-scaffold* &middot; taught in full by **SITHCCC023**

**Why here.** Fifteen of the twenty-five units carry a knowledge-evidence point reading safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment, changing only the dish it names, and fourteen carry the element Select, prepare and use equipment. SITHCCC023 Use food preparation equipment, in Theme 2, is the unit whose entire subject this is. Teaching it fifteen times teaches nothing new fourteen of those times and inflates every downstream assessment tool with a question already answered. The detector suggested SITHKOP009 because it appears first in the unit table, but SITHKOP009 Clean kitchen premises and equipment owns CLEANING equipment, which is a different topic.

**What the other units do.** Every later cookery unit names the equipment its own dishes need and any setting peculiar to them - a blast chiller programme, a combi oven mode, a pastry sheeter gauge - in a few lines. It does not re-teach assembly, safe operation or the manufacturer instruction, and its assessment tool does not re-ask them.

Anchored to training.gov.au:

- SITHCCC023 KE: safe operational practices using essential functions and features of equipment used in the performance evidence.
- SITHCCC023 EL: 2. Use equipment to prepare food.

#### SIT30821-T02 &mdash; Mise en place: what it means and why it governs the kitchen
