## Two things that are not a rename

**5 of the 32 transitions are NON-EQUIVALENT.** The superseding unit is not the same unit with a new number - performance criteria, evidence and assessment conditions changed. Existing assessment tools for these do not transfer and must be rebuilt:

- `SITXINV002` &rarr; `SITXINV006` Receive, store and maintain stock &mdash; *Non-equivalent. Unit supersedes and merges content from two previous units.*
- `SITHCCC018` &rarr; `SITHCCC042` Prepare food to meet special dietary requirements &mdash; *Non-equivalent. Addition of prerequisite. Removal of range of conditions. Changes to A, PC, PE, KE and AC.*
- `SITHPAT006` &rarr; `SITHPAT016` Produce desserts &mdash; *Non-equivalent. Removal of range of conditions. Major changes to PC, PE, KE and AC.*
- `SITHCCC011` &rarr; `SITHCCC034` Work effectively in a commercial kitchen &mdash; *Non-equivalent. Title changed. Addition of prerequisite. Removal of range of conditions. Changes to PE, KE and AC.*
- `SITXINV001` &rarr; `SITXINV006` Receive, store and maintain stock &mdash; *Non-equivalent. Unit supersedes and merges content from two previous units.*

**The unit count collapses from 32 to 31.** `SITXINV001 Receive and store stock` and `SITXINV002 Maintain the quality of perishable items` both supersede to the single unit `SITXINV006 Receive, store and maintain stock`. The packaging rule for SIT40721 requires 32 units, so one further elective has to be chosen. That is a delivery decision and only Meridian can make it.
